# Ulmarin Technology

Ulmarin technology developed under two hard constraints that shaped almost
everything about it: no fire is possible underwater, and no metal ore is
easily reachable from the trenches where the species originated. In place of
combustion and smelting, Ulmarin engineering is built around cultivated
biology — living systems bred and shaped over generations to do the work
other species assign to machines.

Their most important technology is the vent-forge: a colony of heat-tolerant
organisms cultivated directly over a thermal vent, bred over centuries to
concentrate and slowly crystallize dissolved minerals into workable structural
material. A vent-forge is not built so much as grown and tended, sometimes
by the same family lineage for hundreds of years, and losing one to
mismanagement is treated as a genuine civic disaster rather than an
inconvenience.

Communication technology follows the same pattern. Long-distance messages
travel through cultivated vibration-relay reefs — chains of a coral-like
organism that propagate a pressure pulse from node to node across great
distances, functioning much like a telegraph line but grown rather than laid.
Message speed depends on reef health, so reef maintenance is a recognized,
respected profession, and deliberately damaging a relay reef is treated
extremely seriously — closer to sabotaging a colony's ability to speak at all
than a act of ordinary property destruction.

Since the Thaw Accord, first-generation ice-surface technology — light-well
shafts drilled up through the shell to bring surface light into ceiling
settlements — has spread rapidly, and is the closest thing Ulmarin
civilization has to an "imported" technology from a non-Ulmarin cause.
