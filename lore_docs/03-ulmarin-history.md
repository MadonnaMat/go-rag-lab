# Ulmarin History

Ulmarin history is conventionally divided at the Rising — the millennia-long
migration of most lineages up out of the deep trenches toward the sunlit
underside of Vasa Prime's ice shell, where the first permanent surface-lit
settlements were anchored to the ceiling of open sub-ice caverns. Before the
Rising, Ulmarin society was organized entirely around individual trench vents,
each supporting one isolated drift-circle cluster with almost no contact
between vents; the Rising is remembered less as a single event than as a slow
discovery that other trenches, and other Ulmarin, existed at all.

The first sustained contact between surface and deep-trench lineages produced
the Long Argument, a centuries-spanning cultural conflict rather than a
military one, over whether bioluminescence should be preserved or was an
embarrassing relic of a darker, harder past. No side "won" the Long Argument
in any formal sense; it simply cooled into the present-day split between
glow-preserving trench communities and the largely non-luminous surface
cities, a divide Ulmarin now treat as a source of cultural richness rather
than a wound needing to be closed.

The most recent major event, the Thaw Accord, established shared governance
over the ice-shell surface as it began measurably retreating due to increased
tidal heating — the first Ulmarin crisis whose cause originated outside their
own society entirely, and the reason most current political structures are
barely two generations old.
